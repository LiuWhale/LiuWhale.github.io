About

I study safe robot learning and trustworthy embodied AI: how robots can anticipate risk, adapt their actions, and operate reliably under uncertainty.

I am advised by Prof. Changyin Sun at Southeast University and am a joint doctoral researcher with Prof. Shengbo Eben Li's team at Tsinghua University. Previously, I visited MBZUAI, working with Dr. Anqing Duan.

Research

Safe robot learning
Action rectification and safety filters for constrained continuous control.
World-model risk prediction
Diffusion world models and Langevin action search under uncertainty.
Trustworthy embodied AI
Runtime safety for vision-language-action models and robot policies.
Adaptive safety certification
Safety certificates and invariant sets for uncertain robot dynamics.
